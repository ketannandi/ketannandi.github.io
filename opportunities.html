03

Research areas

Your EOI should explain how your interests align with these areas. See the research section and publications for more detail.

01

Braided river eco-morphodynamics

Energy dissipation, channel shifting and stability of large braided rivers such as the Brahmaputra, using entropy-based and process-based indicators.

02

Flow–vegetation interaction

Laboratory flume studies of flow, turbulence and secondary currents around vegetated bars, floodplains and sand-mined channels.

03

Nature-based river corridor management

Vegetation and other nature-based solutions for river stability, restoration and sustainable flood management.

04

Remote sensing, modelling & monitoring

Google Earth Engine, GIS and hydrodynamic modelling, with underwater robotics and sensor-based systems for river surveys and dam safety.

04

How to apply

Four simple steps. All applications are made by email.

Download the EOI form

Get the Expression of Interest form in Word or PDF.

Fill it in & prepare documents

Complete all sections and attach your updated CV, a research proposal or statement of interest (if applicable) and a list of publications (if available).

Email your application

Send everything to ketannandi@gmail.com using the subject line below.

Review

EOIs are reviewed on research alignment, academic background and the availability of suitable opportunities. For a Ph.D., also apply through SR University's admission process.

Expression of Interest – Research Opportunities & Collaboration – [Your Name] Open email →
Submission of an application does not guarantee admission, funding, or an appointment.
